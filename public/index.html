About

Who I teach, and how we start

I work with individuals who want leverage from digital assistants, SMBs wiring them into day-to-day ops, and enterprise teams that need the same ideas to survive security and reliability review.

A first conversation is usually concrete: one workflow that burns time, what a digital worker could own, and what has to be true underneath (access, data, cloud controls) so it does not fall over.

By day I lead cloud cybersecurity work at Check Point. Earlier I built resilient network, wireless, and voice infrastructure. That mix is why I care that the platforms around automation actually hold up.

What a first session covers

  • Pick one high-friction workflow worth automating
  • Map where a digital assistant or worker fits
  • Name the security and reliability constraints early
  • Leave with a small, shippable next step
